Instagram & TikTok

Influencers connect their accounts through Instagram's and TikTok's own sign-in. This is what XPORA receives, and why.

From Instagram

  • Account ID, username and account type
  • Follower count
  • Recent posts, reels and stories, with their numbers

From TikTok

  • User ID, username and display name
  • Follower count
  • Public videos, with their numbers

Used to

  • Confirm the account is yours
  • Understand your audience size
  • Show the business you visited how your content performed

XPORA never posts for you and never sees your password. You can remove its access at any time in Instagram or TikTok. Privacy Policy
