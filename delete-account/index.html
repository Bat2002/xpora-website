Account and Personal Data Deletion

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This page sets out how a User may request the deletion of their Account and of the Personal Data associated with it, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law promulgated by Law No. 151 of 2020 of the Arab Republic of Egypt and its Executive Regulations (the "PDPL"). It is to be read together with the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy, and terms defined in them have the same meaning here. Nothing on this page amends the Terms of Service or the Privacy Policy, which prevail in the event of any inconsistency.

1. Scope

1.1 This page applies to every User of the Platform, whether a Business or an Influencer, and to any person who has submitted an Application that has not resulted in an Account.

2. Deletion through the Platform

2.1 A User may delete their Account at any time, without stating a reason, by taking the following steps in the Platform:

  • open the Profile tab and select Settings;
  • select "Delete Account" at the bottom of the Settings page;
  • confirm the deletion by selecting "Yes".

2.2 Deletion takes effect immediately upon confirmation. The User is signed out of the Platform on all devices, and the deletion cannot be reversed. A User who wishes to use the Platform again must submit a new Application.

3. Conditions for Deletion

3.1 In accordance with clause 11.1 of the Terms of Service, an Account may not be deleted while any of the following subsists:

  • in the case of an Influencer: an active Booking, a pending withdrawal request, or a balance in the Wallet;
  • in the case of a Business: a Request that is pending or in process.

3.2 Any such matter must first be completed, cancelled or withdrawn, as the case may be. These conditions exist to protect the rights of the other Users who rely on the Account, and of the User themselves, in particular so that no amount owed to an Influencer is lost upon deletion.

3.3 Where the balance in an Influencer's Wallet is lower than the minimum withdrawal amount and therefore cannot be withdrawn, the Influencer may submit a deletion request in accordance with clause 4, and XPORA shall contact the Influencer to settle the balance before the Account is deleted.

4. Deletion upon Request

4.1 A User who is unable to access the Platform, or who no longer has the application installed, may request the deletion of their Account by e-mail to support@xporanetwork.com, sent from the e-mail address registered to the Account, bearing the subject "Account Deletion Request", and stating:

  • the full name of the User;
  • the username of the Account;
  • the type of Account (Business or Influencer) and, in the case of a Business, the name of the Business.

4.2 A person who has submitted an Application that has not resulted in an Account may, in the same manner, request the deletion of their Application and of the Personal Data contained in it.

4.3 XPORA may ask the person submitting a request to verify their identity, including by contacting them on the telephone number registered to the Account, before acting on the request. XPORA shall not act on a request whose sender's identity cannot be verified.

4.4 The conditions set out in clause 3 apply equally to deletion upon request. Where any such matter subsists, XPORA shall inform the User of what must be settled before the Account can be deleted.

4.5 XPORA shall process the request within forty-eight (48) hours of receiving it, or any shorter period prescribed by the PDPL, and shall notify the User by e-mail once the deletion has been completed.

5. Data Deleted

5.1 Upon deletion, the Personal Data associated with the Account is deleted, including:

  • the name, username, password, telephone number and e-mail address;
  • the profile picture, or the photographs, description and Branches of a Business;
  • in the case of an Influencer, the connection to their Instagram and TikTok accounts, including the access keys to them;
  • the notification tokens of the User's devices;
  • the Requests, Bookings and notifications associated with the Account;
  • the Wallet and the withdrawal requests, subject to clause 6.

6. Data Retained

6.1 In accordance with clause 11.3 of the Terms of Service and clause 8.2 of the Privacy Policy, XPORA retains, to the extent required to comply with the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt:

(a) in the case of a Business, the name and contact information of the Business and of the person who represented it, including the addresses of its Branches, for the purpose of addressing any complaint, together with the records of its transactions;

(b) in the case of an Influencer, the records of the amounts credited and paid to the Influencer, including the details of each withdrawal, together with the identifying and contact information associated with them.

6.2 Such data is retained for no longer than the applicable law requires, being five (5) years from the date on which the Account is deleted, is accessible only to authorised XPORA personnel, and is not used for any purpose other than that for which it is retained.

6.3 Deletion of an Account does not remove the Content that an Influencer has published on Instagram or TikTok, which is subject to the policies of the operators of those services and to clause 9.3 of the Terms of Service.

7. Deletion of Specific Data

7.1 A User who wishes to have specific Personal Data corrected or erased without deleting their Account may submit a request in accordance with clause 10 of the Privacy Policy. The erasure of data that is necessary to operate the Account may prevent XPORA from continuing to provide the Platform to the User.

8. Complaints

8.1 Without prejudice to the foregoing, a User may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Center.
